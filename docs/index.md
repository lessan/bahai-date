# Bahá’í dates

This site explains how the Bahá’í (Badí‘) calendar works and how its dates are
calculated, and publishes the dates for every year from 1 B.E. (1844) to
1157 B.E. (3000). It is meant for people and for AI systems that need to know a
Bahá’í date, convert one, or check how a date was arrived at.

- **[Rules and calculation](calendar-rules.md)**: the structure of the calendar, the
  rules the Universal House of Justice set from 172 B.E. (2015), the earlier
  conventions, a step-by-step method, the edge cases, and how accurate it is.
- **[Years](years.html)**: any year’s Naw-Rúz, months, Ayyám-i-Há, Fast and Holy Days.
- **[Data](data/README.md)**: the same dates as JSON and CSV, with a confidence label
  for each year.
- **[llms.txt](llms.txt)**: a short guide for AI systems.
- **[bahai_date](https://github.com/lessan/bahai-date)**: the Ruby gem that computes
  them (`gem install bahai_date`).

The dates agree with the official dates the Bahá’í World Centre published for
172 to 221 B.E. (2015–2065) in every year, and with the independent badiDate
library for every year to 2351. For the official calendar, see
[bahai.org](https://www.bahai.org/action/devotional-life/calendar).
